Free, open learning roadmap

Design systems that work in the real world.

Learn system design one topic at a time through concise guides, practical examples, and tests. Start with the foundations, then follow the modules in order—or jump to the topic you need.

Explore the roadmap Contribute Ask and discuss Contribution board

Learn by building understanding

Each module has one canonical repository with its topics organized inside. Most modules include runnable examples and tests. The case studies and interview-prep modules focus on architecture exercises and learning tools.

All material is free to read and use under the repositories' MIT licenses.
